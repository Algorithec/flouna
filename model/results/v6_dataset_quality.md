## Label rules enforced

* **Pickup** is kept only when the place appears in the sentence. 535 V5 rows
  and 3 sealed cases carried a pickup the text never mentioned; those are gone
  from training and remain honest misses in the sealed set.
* **Vehicle** comes from an explicit word (bike/motorbike/scooter,
  auto/rickshaw, cab/taxi/car/uber/ola), otherwise `any`.
* **Dietary** takes an explicit veg/non-veg/meat statement first, then the
  canonical dish table (including dosa, cake, brownie, pancakes, coffee),
  otherwise `any`.
* **Priority** takes the user's cue (rating > speed > price, case-insensitive,
  Hindi/Telugu cues included), then a delivery deadline or pickup wait means
  speed, otherwise balanced. Explicit no-preference wording ("no special
  preference", "decent", "good value") stays balanced even with a deadline.
* **Item** is the dish if named, else the meal word, else the taste phrase,
  else `food`.
* **Shop category** is the canonical item-to-category map; the enum is
  unchanged at electronics/fashion/home/appliances/any.
* **Action** fields follow the server contract: 1-based targetPosition,
  `none`/0/""/false for unused fields, weeklyRupees 0 removes the budget,
  password/two-factor/fingerprint/sign-out/delete are navigate security.
